## 📌 Turnover & Operations Guide

**To the StoryFirst PH Team,**

We are pleased to hand over the finalized StoryFirst PH website. The platform is currently live, fully functional, and resting securely on a permanent, free hosting environment until you are ready to officially launch it to the public.

Because the team does not have an IT background, we have engineered this website so that **zero coding is required** to manage, edit, or update it moving forward.

Here is a simple, non-technical guide on how your new platform works and how to manage it.

---

### 1. How the Website Works (The Big Picture)

Think of the website in two parts: **The House** and **The Furniture**.

- **The House (GitHub Pages):** This is the foundation and structure of the website. It is where the code lives and how the website stays online. You will rarely, if ever, need to touch this.
- **The Furniture (Sanity CMS):** This is your content dashboard. Whenever you want to change a photo, update a project description, or add a new team member, you will do it here. When you change the "furniture," the "house" automatically updates.

---

### 2. Managing Your Content (Sanity CMS)

You have a dedicated, private dashboard to manage the website.

- **How to edit:** Simply log in to the Sanity Studio using the link at the top of this document. You will see a clean menu with your website sections (Projects, Clients, Advocacies, Team, etc.).
- **Publishing:** When you make a change (like fixing a typo or adding a new partner) and hit "Publish" in the dashboard, the live website will automatically update within 1 to 2 minutes.
- **The Archive Feature:** If you want to temporarily hide a project, team member, or advocacy from the live website without deleting their data, just toggle the **"Archive (Hide from website)"** switch in the dashboard.
- **Video Pop-ups:** When adding videos to your Projects or Notable Collabs, you cannot use a standard browser link. You must use the official **Embed URL** (e.g., `https://www.youtube.com/embed/...` or the Facebook `video.php?href=...` format).

---

### 3. Client Inquiries & Bookings (Fully Editable)

You have total control over where your emails and bookings go, and you can change them at any time directly from the dashboard.

- **Contact Form:** The website’s contact form is fully active and currently routes to StoryFirst PH email. **If you need to change the receiving email address in the future:** You simply go to Web3Forms.com, generate a new free "Access Key" using the new email address, and paste that key into the **Contact Settings** in your Sanity dashboard.
- **Free Consultations:** The "Book a Free Consultation" button is linked to your current Calendly account. **If you change your Calendly account:** Just paste your new Calendly link into the **Services Settings** in the dashboard. If you ever want to disable the booking feature entirely, simply delete the link from the dashboard, and the booking button will automatically disappear from the live website.

---

### 4. Zero Recurring Costs

We have specifically built this website using modern "serverless" technology to eliminate monthly maintenance fees.

- **Hosting:** Free forever via GitHub Pages.
- **Content Management:** Free forever via Sanity's generous base tier.
- **Email Routing:** Free forever via Web3Forms (up to 250 inquiries a month).
- **Bookings:** Free forever via Calendly's basic plan.
- _Conclusion:_ You can let this website sit exactly as it is for a year or more, and you will not be charged a single peso.

---

### 5. Critical Security Note (Keep These Safe)

Because you do not have an IT department, the most important thing you must do is **secure your login credentials**. If you lose access to these accounts, you lose control of the website:

1. **The GitHub Account:** Holds the actual website structure and hosting.
2. **The Sanity Account:** Holds all your text, images, and content.
3. **The Gmail Account:** Receives all client inquiries from the contact form.
